# Organizations

An organization represents a collective name for groups of apps, blocks and platform users. In order
to publish new blocks or to create and manage apps, the platform user needs to be in an
organization.

## Creating an organization

Organizations can be freely made in the Appsemble studio by visiting the settings page and clicking
on “Organizations” in the menu. From here you can view a list of organizations that you are already
part of, as well as create new organizations. When creating a new organization note that the
organization ID **must** be unique and cannot be changed after it has been created. This ID is used
to determine which blocks are used in an app, as well as where the app should be hosted by default.

## Deleting an organization

Organizations can be deleted by visiting the settings page of the respective organization and
clicking on the “Edit Organization” button. Only the owner of the organization is allowed to delete
the organization. In addition to that, the organization shouldn’t have any published blocks for it
to be deleted. Clicking on the `delete` button will open a popup for confirmation, following the
respective steps will allow you to delete the organization.

## Organization members

After creating an organization, members can be added via email. This email will contain a link that
the recipient can click on in order to accept or decline joining the organization.

Organization members can be assigned roles that affect which parts of the platform they have access
to. These roles are the following:

- `Member`: The default role for new organization members. Organization members are able to view
  private apps that belong to the organization.
- `AppTranslator`: App translators can view and modify app messages.
- `AppContentsExplorer`: App contents explorers can view app resources and assets.
- `AppContentsManager`: App contents managers can view and modify app resources and assets.
- `AppMemberManager`: App member managers can invite new members to an app and modify existing app
  members’ roles and properties.
- `AppGroupMembersManager`: App group members managers can invite exising app members to groups and
  modify existing group members’ roles.
- `AppGroupManager`: App group managers have all permissions of `AppGroupMembersManager`. They can
  also create new groups and modify exising ones.
- `AppManager`: App managers have the permissions of all of the above roles combined. They can also
  view and modify app variables, secrets and settings.
- `AppCollectionManager`: App collection managers can create and modify app collections.
- `BlockManager`: Block managers can publish and delete organization blocks.
- `Maintainer`: Maintainers can create and delete apps. They can also create and manage organization
  invites.
- `Owner`: Owners can update and delete organizations. They can also remove members and update their
  roles.

## Service accounts

Owners create [service accounts](service-accounts.md) on the **Members** page for CI pipelines and
other automation that uses the API on behalf of the organization.

## Single sign-on

Owners configure OpenID Connect on the organization's **Single sign-on** page. Register a
confidential client with the identity provider and use `https://<studio-host>/callback` as its
redirect URI and `https://<studio-host>/` as its post-logout redirect URI. Enter the issuer URL,
client ID and client secret. The issuer must use HTTPS and be one the installation operator allows.
The scope includes `openid`; the default scope is `openid email profile` and the default group claim
is `groups`. The provider sends the email address, `email_verified` and the group claim, with
Keycloak groups as full paths.

Map provider groups to the organization roles listed above. Group values match exactly, including
paths such as `/appsemble/maintainers`. A staff member receives the combined permissions of all
matching roles across the organization. Group-managed roles are controlled by the provider;
unmatched staff receive no organization access. Signing in through the organization's provider turns
a member whose groups match into group-managed staff, except that the organization's last Owner
keeps a manual Owner role.

The organization selects a sign-in policy:

- **Allow single sign-on and other permitted methods** lets manually managed members use the
  selected other sign-in methods.
- **Require organization single sign-on for everyone** requires the organization's provider.
- **Require single sign-on for staff; allow invited external guests** lets explicitly invited guests
  use the selected other sign-in methods. Only Owners invite guests, and losing a staff group does
  not create a guest exception.

The policy does not apply to [service accounts](service-accounts.md), which only use the API with
client credentials.

Select **Verify connection and save** and sign in with a provider account whose groups grant Owner
access. The connection is saved after successful verification, and unsuccessful verification keeps
the active connection intact.

Staff sign in at `https://<studio-host>/login?organization=<organization id>` or by entering their
email address on the Studio sign-in page. An email address that belongs to an existing Appsemble
account, whether entered on the sign-in page or sent by the provider, must be linked first: sign in
to that account, then link organization single sign-on. A matching email address alone does not link
accounts. Password recovery and other linked sign-in methods cannot bypass an organization's
required single sign-on, and a sign-in through another organization's provider does not count for
this organization. Signing out of the Studio also signs out at a provider which offers an
`end_session_endpoint` and then returns to the Studio, so the next sign-in asks for credentials
again.

Manage linked organization identities under **Settings > Social login**, which shows each identity's
organizations, their provider names and the provider account: the email address from the last
sign-in through the provider, or its preferred username without one. Owners see the provider
accounts of linked members on the **Members** page. **Unlink single sign-on** requires recent
authentication and a verified recovery email or personal social login. Unlinking ends that
identity's sessions and revokes its personal CLI credentials while keeping other linked identities,
manual memberships and guest grants. An organization must retain an Owner. To link the identity
again, sign in to the existing Appsemble account and use the organization's sign-in page.

Provider groups are refreshed when the provider access token expires, at most one hour after the
previous refresh, and a Studio session ends after eight hours. Removing a group therefore takes
effect at the next refresh, including for personal CLI credentials.

Registering personal client credentials in a session from the organization's provider asks the
provider for offline access, so the client must allow the `offline_access` scope. The credentials
use that grant of their own: they keep working after signing out, and stop when the provider ends
the grant, disables the account or removes its groups. The client credentials settings show
credentials whose grant has ended; register new ones to replace them.

Removing the single sign-on configuration deletes group-managed memberships and keeps manual and
guest memberships; the removing Owner keeps the Owner role. It ends all sessions through the
provider. Accounts that only sign in through the provider regain access through password recovery
for their verified email address. Keep a personal password or social login for account recovery
because the organization controls access through its single sign-on provider.

Operators can declare organization single sign-on through the [Helm chart](../deployment/helm.md).
Deployment-managed settings are read-only in Studio. Operators recover access by correcting the
declared configuration; account recovery does not disable the organization's sign-in policy.
